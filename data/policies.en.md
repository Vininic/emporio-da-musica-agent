# Empório da Música: Policies and Procedures Manual

English translation of `data/policies.md`, kept faithful to the original, including where it contradicts itself.

## 1 About Empório da Música

Empório da Música is a store specialized in musical instruments, founded in 2008 in Campo Grande, Mato Grosso do Sul. With over 15 years in the market, we are a regional reference in selling instruments to beginner, intermediate and professional musicians. Our catalog covers more than 300 instruments from national and international brands, including electric guitars, basses, acoustic guitars, drums, keyboards, wind instruments, orchestral strings and ukuleles. We work exclusively with musical instruments: we do not sell accessories such as strings, picks, cables, cases, pedals or amplifiers.

## 1.1 Mission

To democratize access to quality musical instruments in the Center-West region, offering specialized service and competitive prices, contributing to the cultural and musical development of the community.

## 2 Opening Hours

In-store service follows the hours below. WhatsApp service follows the same hours, with automatic replies outside business hours. Monday to Friday: 09:00 to 18:00. Saturday: 09:00 to 13:00. Sundays and holidays: closed. On special dates (Black Friday, Store Anniversary, Christmas), hours may be extended. Changes are announced in advance on social media and on WhatsApp.

## 3 Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods for in-store and online purchases. PIX: paid in full, 5% discount on the list price. Debit card: paid in full, all card brands accepted. Credit card: up to 12 interest-free installments, minimum installment of R$ 100.00. Bank slip (boleto): paid in full, clears within 3 business days.

## 3.1 Installment Rules

Up to 3 installments: interest free, no minimum installment (except below R$ 50.00). 4 to 6 installments: interest free, minimum installment of R$ 80.00. 7 to 12 installments: interest free, minimum installment of R$ 100.00. Combining payment methods: allowed (e.g. PIX + card) for purchases above R$ 2,000.00.

## 4 Exchange and Return Policy

Empório da Música follows the Consumer Protection Code (Law 8.078/90) and offers clear conditions for exchanges and returns.

## 4.1 Right of Withdrawal (Online Purchases)

The customer may request a return within 7 (seven) calendar days after receiving the product, with no need for justification. The product must be in its original packaging, with no signs of use, with all accessories and manuals. The refund is made through the original payment method within 10 business days. Return shipping is paid by the store in the case of withdrawal.

## 4.2 Exchanges for Defects

Products with manufacturing defects may be exchanged within 30 (thirty) calendar days after purchase. After 30 days, the customer must claim the warranty directly with the manufacturer. Empório da Música may mediate the process on request. Damage caused by misuse, falls, excessive humidity or unauthorized modifications is not covered.

## 4.3 Preference Exchanges

Exchanges for preference (color, model, size) are allowed within 7 days, subject to availability of the desired product. The product must be in perfect condition and in its original packaging. Price differences are charged or refunded as applicable.

## 4.4 Items Not Eligible for Exchange

Instruments with customization or made-to-order adjustments (setup, special regulation). Products bought in clearance promotions with an explicit "final sale" notice. Mouthpieces of wind instruments, for hygiene reasons.

## 5.1 Deliveries in the Campo Grande Metropolitan Area

Free shipping for orders above R$ 500.00. For orders below R$ 500.00, a flat fee of R$ 35.00. Delivery time: 1 to 3 business days. Delivered by the store's own courier. The customer is called before delivery.

## 5.2 Deliveries to Other Cities

We use Correios (PAC and SEDEX) and the carrier Jadlog for shipments outside the Campo Grande metropolitan area. PAC (Correios): 5 to 12 business days, with tracking and insurance included. SEDEX (Correios): 2 to 5 business days, with tracking and insurance included. Jadlog (.package): 3 to 8 business days, with tracking and insurance included. The shipping cost is calculated automatically from the destination ZIP code, weight and size of the product. Large instruments (acoustic drums, digital pianos, double basses) may require special shipping with an individual quote. All shipments include insurance against loss and damage in transit. In case of damage, the customer must refuse delivery and contact the store immediately.

## 5.3 Tracking Code

The tracking code is sent automatically by e-mail and WhatsApp as soon as the order ships. The standard format is BR followed by 9 alphanumeric characters and BR (example: BR4K7M2X9P1BR). Tracking can be checked directly on the Correios or Jadlog website.

## 6.1 Types of Promotion

Empório da Música runs periodic promotions throughout the year. The main campaigns include: Store Anniversary (August): 10% to 25% off selected items. Black Friday (November): 15% to 30% off the whole catalog. Back to School (February): special discounts on instruments for students. Stock Clearance: occasional promotions to renew the catalog. Musician's Week: promotions in the week of Musician's Day (November 22).

## 6.2 Promotion Rules

Stacking: promotions do not stack. The PIX discount (5%) does not apply to prices already on promotion. Stock: promotions are subject to stock availability. Products that sell out during a promotion do not entitle the customer to a rain check. Communication: promotional prices must always be shown with the original price and the discount percentage, for full transparency.

## 7 WhatsApp Service

The WhatsApp channel (67) 3321-4500 is Empório da Música's main remote service channel. Service may be provided by human agents or by the virtual assistant.

## 7.1 Service Guidelines

Tone of voice: informal but professional. The customer should feel welcome, as if talking to a friend who knows music. Avoid overly formal or robotic language. Response time: at most 5 minutes during business hours. Outside business hours, the virtual assistant must state when service returns. Accurate information: never give information on prices, stock or deadlines without checking the system. Incorrect information causes frustration and may constitute false advertising. Scope: the agent must state that the store works exclusively with musical instruments. Requests for accessories (strings, cables, picks, pedals, amplifiers, cases) must be politely redirected. Suggest partner stores when possible.

## 7.2 Standard Service Flow

Greeting: greet the customer by name (if available) and ask how to help. Understanding: identify the customer's need (product search, question, tracking, complaint). Lookup: search the system for the relevant information (product, stock, promotion, order). Answer: present the options clearly and objectively, with prices and conditions. Closing: confirm whether the customer needs anything else and close courteously.

## 7.3 Special Situations

Out of stock: say the product is temporarily unavailable and suggest similar available alternatives. Never confirm availability without checking current stock. Discontinued: say the product is no longer in the catalog and offer equivalent models or successors. Expired promotion: if the customer asks about a promotion, check whether it is active in the system. If it has expired, say so openly and give the current price. Never promise a discount no longer in effect. Complaint: listen to the customer with empathy, record the complaint and forward it to the person responsible. Response time: 24 business hours.

## 8.1 Legal Warranty

Under the Consumer Protection Code, all products have a legal warranty of 90 (ninety) days against manufacturing defects, counted from the date the customer receives them.

## 8.2 Manufacturer's Warranty

Beyond the legal warranty, most manufacturers offer their own warranty, ranging from 6 months to 2 years. Specific terms and conditions are in the warranty certificate that comes with each product.

## 8.3 What the Warranty Does Not Cover

Natural wear of parts (frets, strings, felts, wind reeds). Damage from misuse, falls, or extreme weather conditions. Modifications or repairs by unauthorized third parties. Cosmetic damage that does not affect the instrument's function.

## 9 Privacy and Data Protection

Empório da Música complies with the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018). Personal data collected during service (name, phone, e-mail, address) is used exclusively for: processing and delivering orders; communication about order status and tracking; sending promotions and news (with the customer's explicit consent); legal and tax obligations. Customer data is not shared with third parties for marketing. The customer may request deletion of their data at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp or by e-mail.

## 10 Final Provisions

This manual is an internal document of Empório da Música and must be used as a reference by all service team members, including the virtual assistant. The policies in this document are subject to periodic updates. When in doubt about applying any policy, the agent must consult management before giving information to the customer. The goal is always transparency, accuracy and the best possible customer experience.
